    AT HOME WITH ECHO

    Your home, connected.

    DEVICE ACCESS

    Choose what Echo can see and control, and give your devices rooms that make sense to you.

    Room lights

    Assign bulbs below to Bedroom, Living Room, Dining Room or Patio, then save. These rooms appear on the round display too. A room button controls all its assigned lights. Agent Control permission is separate.

    Need to find a bulb? Filter to Lights and use its Turn on / Turn off buttons, then choose its room. These buttons change only that bulb and leave it in the state you choose. Save room edits before using the controls.

    Reading Home Assistant…

    Choose Control for each device Echo may change when you ask. Voice commands can use those devices; text chat also requires Allow home actions for that message. Echo checks current state and reports what it can verify. Existing thermostat and Bose controls on the display keep their current behavior.

    Applies to devices without a saved override, including newly discovered devices.

    Device access and Echo rooms are encrypted locally. Hiding a device removes it from future tool results and clears recent chat context; it does not erase facts you explicitly saved in Memory.

    THE LITTLE THINGS THAT MATTER

    A little more you.

    MEMORY

    Facts and preferences you asked Echo to remember. Shared with the device, kept across restarts, and always yours to change.

    Changing memory clears recent conversation context so an old reply cannot bring a deleted fact back. Exported files contain your saved facts as readable text.
