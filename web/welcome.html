REQUIRED

Create your Personal account

This gives you separate saved facts, personality and conversations on screens you choose. It is an Echo account, not a Google account.

Save your one-time passcode

Use these eight digits to unlock your Personal space on an approved Deck, Mini or browser. Echo will not show this code again. Keep it private.

If you lose it, the owner can reset it in Deck Settings → People & access → Manage accounts.

OPTIONAL

Bring in your Google calendars

Echo uses a Google authorization page; it never asks for your Google password. The owner must configure this installation's OAuth app once. Then unlock your Echo Personal account on the calendar page and connect your Google account for read-only access.

Connecting an account does not select calendars automatically. Choose the calendars you want after sign-in. If you lost your Echo passcode, reset it under Settings → People & access → Manage accounts.
